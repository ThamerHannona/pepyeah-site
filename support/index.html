Support — PEP YEAH

Need help? Email support@pepyeah.com.

For faster support, include your device (iPhone model), iOS version, and what you were doing when the issue happened.

Account and sign-in

  • Sign in options: Apple, Google, or email (depending on your app build).
  • Trouble signing in: confirm you’re using the same method you originally used.

Subscriptions (iOS)

PEP YEAH Pro subscriptions on iOS are purchased through Apple In‑App Purchase.

  • Manage/cancel: open iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
  • Trials: if a free trial is offered, cancel before the trial ends to avoid charges.
  • Deleting the app: removing the app does not cancel your Apple subscription.

Exporting and deleting data

  • Exports: the app may offer exports such as PDF reports, depending on feature availability.
  • Delete account: use the in-app delete option (if available) or email support@pepyeah.com.

Safety note

PEP YEAH is a tracking tool. It does not prescribe, source compounds, diagnose conditions, or recommend dose changes. If you have health concerns, consult a qualified clinician.
