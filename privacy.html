Privacy Policy — PEP YEAH

Effective date: September 8, 2026

Last updated: September 17, 2026

PEP YEAH (“PEP YEAH,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a protocol tracking app operated by Thamer Hannona under the Shake and Bake Labs brand. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect information when you use the PEP YEAH mobile application and related sites including pepyeah.com (collectively, the “Service”).

This is not legal advice. If you need a lawyer-reviewed policy for your specific situation, have counsel review this policy before relying on it.

1. Who we are

  • Service: PEP YEAH — peptide / protocol tracking companion
  • Operator: Shake and Bake Labs (brand) / Thamer Hannona (individual operator)
  • Contact: support@pepyeah.com
  • Website: pepyeah.com

2. What PEP YEAH is (and is not)

PEP YEAH helps you track and organize information you enter about protocols, doses, vials, schedules, body-map injection sites, reminders, nutrition, side effects, and related notes. Optional features may include an AI coach that summarizes your own records, push reminders, and photos you choose to upload (for example meal and label photos for nutrition features, and progress photos).

PEP YEAH does not prescribe medication, source compounds, diagnose conditions, or recommend dose changes. It is a personal tracking tool, not a medical device or substitute for professional care.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Account information

When you create or sign in to an account, we may collect:

  • Email address
  • Password (stored hashed / handled by our auth provider; we do not store plaintext passwords)
  • Name or display name you provide
  • Authentication identifiers if you use Sign in with Apple or Google (tokens / subject IDs from those providers)

3.2 Protocol and health-related content you enter

Depending on what you use, this may include:

  • Peptide / protocol names, doses, schedules, and frequencies
  • Vial inventory, lot/expiry notes, reconstitution inputs
  • Dose logs (time, amount, injection site, notes)
  • Side-effect logs
  • Weight, sleep, hydration, meal notes or photos
  • Lab / bloodwork values you enter
  • Free-text notes and chat messages you send to Pep Coach

You control what you enter. Do not enter information you are not comfortable storing with us.

3.3 Device and technical data

  • Device type, OS version, app version
  • Push notification device tokens (to send reminders)
  • Approximate diagnostics / crash / performance logs
  • IP address and basic usage logs for security and reliability

3.4 Photos and camera

If you use photo features, we collect the images you choose to upload, which may include meal photos, nutrition label photos, and progress photos. Camera / photo library access is only used for those features and requires your permission on device.

3.5 Purchases and subscriptions

If you subscribe (for example via Apple In-App Purchase or another payment provider), we receive purchase status, product identifiers, and transaction metadata needed to unlock features. Payment card details are processed by Apple or the payment provider — not stored by us on our servers as full card numbers.

Important: deleting the app does not automatically cancel an Apple subscription. Apple subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID settings.

3.6 Website

On pepyeah.com we may collect standard web analytics (pages viewed, referrer, device/browser) and any information you submit through contact forms.

4. How we use information

We use information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Service
  • Authenticate you and secure your account
  • Sync your data across devices
  • Send push notifications and reminders you enable
  • Power Pep Coach summaries based on your logged data (within product guardrails)
  • Process subscriptions and customer support
  • Detect abuse, fraud, and technical issues
  • Comply with law and enforce our terms

We do not use your protocol or health-related content to train public AI models for unrelated third parties, and we do not sell your personal information.

5. AI features (Pep Coach)

Pep Coach may process the records and messages you provide to generate summaries and pattern explanations.

  • Outputs are informational only and are not medical advice.
  • Do not rely on Pep Coach for dosing, sourcing, diagnosis, or treatment decisions.
  • Processing may occur on our servers / subprocessors that host the AI feature.

6. How we share information

We share information only as needed to run the Service:

  • Infrastructure / backend providers (for example cloud hosting and database such as Supabase) to store and serve your account data
  • Authentication providers (Apple, Google) when you choose those sign-in methods
  • Push notification services (for example Apple Push Notification service)
  • Analytics / crash tools if enabled (aggregated or limited technical data)
  • Payment processors (Apple / others) for subscriptions
  • Legal / safety — if required by law, legal process, or to protect rights, safety, and security

We do not sell personal information.

7. Data retention

We retain account and logged content while your account is active. If you delete your account or request deletion, we delete or anonymize personal data within a reasonable period, except where we must retain limited records for legal, security, accounting, or dispute reasons.

You may export certain records where the product provides export (for example CSV), subject to feature availability.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete personal information, or to object to / restrict certain processing.

In-app / account: use available profile or settings controls to update information, manage notifications, export reports (where available), or delete your account where offered.

By email: contact support@pepyeah.com with “Privacy Request” in the subject line.

Deleting the app: removing the app from your phone does not delete your account data by itself. Use the in-app delete option (if available) or email us.

Subscriptions: subscriptions purchased through Apple are managed and canceled in your Apple ID subscription settings.

You can also revoke OS permissions (camera, photos, notifications) in your device settings. That may limit features.

California / similar laws: we do not sell personal information. You may request know/delete/correct as applicable by emailing us.

EEA/UK users: we process data to provide the Service (contract), with consent where required (e.g. certain device permissions), and for legitimate interests such as security — balanced against your rights.

9. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards appropriate to a consumer app (encryption in transit, access controls, authenticated accounts). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. Please use a strong unique password and protect your devices.

10. Children’s privacy

The Service is intended for adults and is not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a minor has provided information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

11. International transfers

Your information may be processed in the United States or other countries where we or our providers operate. Where required, we use appropriate transfer mechanisms.

12. Third-party links and services

The Service may link to third-party sites or services (for example educational content). Their privacy practices are their own. Review their policies before providing information.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page and change the “Last updated” date. Material changes may also be communicated in-app or by email when appropriate.
